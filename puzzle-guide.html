🧩 PUZZLE GUIDE

How to Answer Chess Puzzles

Learn how to read and enter chess moves using simple chess notation. Follow each lesson and you'll be ready to answer Puzzle Events with confidence.

📖 GETTING STARTED

Before You Start

LESSON 1

Learn the Chessboard Squares

Every square on a chessboard has a name. The letters a–h identify the columns, while the numbers 1–8 identify the rows.

For example, the square where the letter e and number 4 meet is called e4.

♟️ CHESS PIECES

Learn the Piece Letters

LESSON 2

Every Piece Has a Letter

In chess notation, most pieces are represented by a letter.

K King
Q Queen
R Rook
B Bishop
N Knight

Pawns do not use a letter. Knights use N because K is already used for the King.

♙ PAWN MOVES

How to Write a Pawn Move

LESSON 3

Pawn Moves Are Simple

When a pawn moves, you normally write only the square it moves to.

e4

This means the pawn moved to the e4 square.

♞ PIECE MOVES

How to Write a Piece Move

LESSON 4

Combine the Piece Letter and Square

For pieces such as the Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen and King, write the piece letter followed by the destination square.

Nf3 Knight moves to f3
Bc4 Bishop moves to c4
Qh5 Queen moves to h5
⚔️ CAPTURES

How to Write a Capture

LESSON 5

What Does X Mean?

The letter x means that the piece is capturing another piece.

Nxf7

This means the Knight captures a piece on f7.

For a pawn capture, include the pawn's starting file.

exd5
⚡ CHECK

How to Write Check

LESSON 6

The + Symbol Means Check

If your move attacks the opponent's King, add a + after the move.

Qh5+

This means the Queen moved to h5 and the move gives check.

♛ CHECKMATE

How to Write Checkmate

LESSON 7

The # Symbol Means Checkmate

If your move puts the King in check and there is no legal way to escape, the move is checkmate.

Qh7#

The # tells you that the move delivers checkmate.

🏰 CASTLING

How to Write Castling

LESSON 8

Castling Has Its Own Notation

Kingside castling is written as:

O-O

Queenside castling is written as:

O-O-O
🧠 PUT IT TOGETHER

You're Ready!

Enter Your Move Correctly

Look at the position, find the best move, and write it using chess notation.

e4 Pawn move
Nf3 Piece move
Bxc6 Capture
Qh5+ Check
Qh7# Checkmate

Once you understand these symbols, you'll be able to enter your answers in Chess News Hub Puzzle Events.
